What the score measures

Readiness is more than profit. The score combines financial numbers with the practices that make a business easier to finance.

25 pts

Financial Health

Liquidity, operating profitability and leverage.

20 pts

Cash-Flow Management

Cash-flow coverage, cash reserve and how often cash flow is monitored.

20 pts

Records & Transparency

Seven recommended record-keeping practices, such as formal accounts and separate bank accounts.

20 pts

Debt & Repayment Capacity

15 pts

Financial Planning

Six planning practices, including budgets, forecasts and an emergency plan.

Methodology and limits

This is an internally designed prototype readiness index built to support decisions and learning. It is not a credit score, a loan-approval model, or a prediction of whether financing will be approved.

The weights, thresholds and classification bands are transparent design choices informed by SME-finance literature. They have not yet been empirically validated, so results should not be used for real lending, investment or regulatory decisions.

The debt-service measure is a cash-flow proxy, not a formal lender debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR).
